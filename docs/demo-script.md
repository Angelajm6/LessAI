# LessAI Demo Script

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 | THE PROBLEM
*(Show lessai.io landing page)*

"Your company is paying for ChatGPT, Claude, Notion AI, Copilot — the whole stack. But nobody trained your team on how to actually use them. 70% of employees have received zero formal AI training. The result? 95% of corporate AI pilots produce no measurable business impact. The problem isn't the tools. It's that nobody teaches people how to talk to AI to get the results they want. That's exactly what LessAI fixes."

---

## 0:20 – 0:50 | ONBOARDING
*(Screen record the onboarding flow)*

"When you sign up, LessAI asks three things: your job title, which AI tools you use, and your company URL."

"Then we scrape your company website to understand your business — your industry, your product, your niche."

*(Show the scraping step)*

"This is what makes LessAI different from any generic AI course. Every prompt, every task, every recommendation is built around your actual job — not a made-up example."

*(Land on the dashboard)*

"And just like that — you have a fully personalized AI coaching hub."

---

## 0:50 – 1:30 | DAILY TASKS

"Every day from Monday to Friday, LessAI gives you one focused practice task for each AI tool in your stack — matched to your current skill level."

*(Click on today's task)*

"Today's task for ChatGPT: write a cold outreach email with full role context. This isn't a made-up exercise — it's something you'd actually do in your job."

*(Mark it complete)*

"Complete it, earn XP, and build your streak. The habit is the product. Consistent daily practice beats any workshop."

*(Show XP/streak update)*

"You can see your level, your streak, and your progress — all in one place."

---

## 1:30 – 2:15 | PROMPT STUDIO

"This is where the real skill-building happens. Prompt Studio has two modes."

*(Switch to Prompt Lab)*

"First — the Prompt Lab. You paste any prompt you've been using and get an AI-rewritten version instantly."

*(Paste a weak prompt)*

"Watch what happens. Before: Specificity 2, Context 1, Output Clarity 3. That's a weak prompt. After:"

*(Show the improved version and scores)*

"Specificity 9, Context 8, Output Clarity 9. And you can see exactly what changed and why — so next time, you write it better yourself."

*(Switch to AI Command Center)*

"The second mode is the AI Command Center. You describe any work task in plain English—"

*(Type a task)*

"—and LessAI tells you which tool to use, exactly why it beats the alternatives for this specific task, and gives you a ready-to-paste prompt built for your role. No more opening five tabs and guessing."

---

## 2:15 – 2:40 | SAVED PROMPTS

"Every prompt you improve or generate can be saved to your personal library — organized into folders, searchable, ready to copy and reuse anytime."

*(Show a folder with prompts)*

"Your best prompts stop living in random docs and Slack messages. They live here."

## 2:40 – 3:10 | ADMIN DASHBOARD

"Now — for managers and team leads."

*(Show the team leaderboard)*

"The admin dashboard shows you every team member's XP, streak, level, and tasks completed — in real time. You can see immediately who's building the habit and who needs a nudge."

*(Point to the flagged members)*

"These two members haven't practiced in over a week. You know exactly who to coach — without running a single survey."

"This is the data that lets you walk into a leadership meeting and prove your AI investment is paying off."

## 3:10 – 3:30 | CLOSE
*(Back to lessai.io or end card)*

"LessAI is live today at lessai.io. You can access a full working demo without creating an account — everything you just saw is clickable and real."

"We didn't build another AI tool. We built the missing layer between the tools your company already pays for and the people who are supposed to use them."
